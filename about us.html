Our Story

Where It Started

We started with a simple belief: the most meaningful
pieces aren't always new. A worn leather bag, achain passed
down, a fabric with a past these things carry more character than anything fresh off a shelf.


What began as a handful of reworked pieces made for
friends has grown into a small collection, each item reclaimed,
reshaped, and given a second life worth carrying every day.

01

Reclaimed

Every material starts somewhere
else a bag, a fabric, a story we didn't write
but got to continue.

02

Handcrafted

Each piece is shaped by hand, in small
batches, so no two are ever quite the same.

03

Handcrafted

We build for years of wear, not one
season pieces meant to age well and be handed
down.

A Word From Our Founder

"I didn't want to sell things people would forget

Founder
